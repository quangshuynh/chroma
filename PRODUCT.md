# Chroma product context

Chroma is a native macOS raster image editor for people who want paint.net-like approachability in a real Mac application. The current scope includes the image/document foundation, editable raster layers, normal-alpha compositing, undo/redo, and native layered saving alongside flattened export. Pencil, Brush, Eraser, and composited Eyedropper support pixel editing with stroke-level undo, native color controls, and adjustable document-pixel size. Binary rectangle/ellipse selections constrain painting and support inversion, pixel translation, clipboard operations, deletion, and undoable crop across layers.

The user's brief fixes the visual direction: restrained native controls, system appearance, SF Symbols, top controls, a central workspace, room for future tools on the left and inspectors on the right. Native behavior, clarity, accessibility, and image fidelity take precedence over decoration. Do not add placeholder tools or History controls.
