# Chroma product context

Chroma is a native macOS raster image editor for people who want paint.net-like approachability in a real Mac application. The current scope is the image/document foundation: create, open, view, navigate, inspect dimensions, and safely save or export. Editing pixels is future work.

The user's brief fixes the visual direction: restrained native controls, system appearance, SF Symbols, top controls, a central workspace, room for future tools on the left and inspectors on the right. Native behavior, clarity, accessibility, and image fidelity take precedence over decoration. Do not add placeholder tool or Layers/History controls.
